Title: About Sleepwalker

[Read HTML](https://sleepwalker.in/about/)

Seven children encounter an impossible book. Years later, with the book lost and their lives scattered, dreams of ruins on distant worlds draw them back together.

Sleepwalker is a mythic science-fiction graphic novel. Its questions about time, memory and consciousness also find room in an inquiry and in methods of making: reading a passage closely, following what a witness can know, or discovering how a page turn changes a scene.

[Harsh Joshi](https://getharsh.in/about/) (on getHarsh) is developing the story and its world. Sleepwalker is a project of RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited (RTEPL).

## Reading the Vedic world closely

A familiar figure can enter a different relationship in another account. A passage can resist the meaning a reader hoped to borrow from it. Following those differences opens questions about time, attention, consciousness and devotion.

Read [Markandeya’s encounters](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/vedic-world-inquiry/through-markandeyas-encounters/) across adjacent chapters, or follow [a sound towards silence](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/vedic-world-inquiry/following-a-symbol-through-its-transformations/). Particular accounts, translations and interpretations give the inquiry its substance.

## Making the story into a page

A character must remain recognisable when the view changes. Moving a speech balloon can change who witnessed an event. A page turn can reveal a relationship that no individual image contained.

The creative methods work through these choices in [image and sequence](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/creative-methods/from-frame-to-sequence/) and [lettering](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/creative-methods/words-and-the-space-around-them/). Generative workflows make alternatives easier to explore; composition, controlled refinement and reading at the intended size give those alternatives a deliberate form.

[Creative methods](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/creative-methods/)

## Different ways into the same questions

The story lets a reader encounter a world before being given an explanation for it. The essays of the Vedic-world inquiry can stay with a passage or a question, while the creative methods examine the decisions that give an encounter its form. Each can be read independently. A traditional account may inform a scene without becoming its script; a method may suggest a technique without becoming a finished page. These crossings are part of how Sleepwalker is taking shape.
