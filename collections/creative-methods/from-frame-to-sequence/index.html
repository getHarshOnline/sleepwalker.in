  1. Creative methods

From frame to sequence

Carry a visual choice through character continuity into the reader's inference between panels.

A compelling frame offers a relationship worth keeping: a figure's scale, an opening around it, a line of light. Following it into another image asks which parts can vary while the person and place remain recognisable. Following those images onto a page asks what the reader can infer between them.

That progression changes the unit of the work. The individual image becomes a continuous presence, then a sequence whose meaning exceeds any one frame. What began as a visual choice becomes an experience someone else completes.

Explore this sequence

  1. An image is a beginning A generated frame reveals a relation worth keeping. Composition, spatial conditioning and refinement give that discovery a way to develop.
  2. Keeping a character through a sequence A person approaches a doorway through changing views and material registers; recognition makes the variation expressive.
  3. What happens between the panels A door opens between two images. Panel order, withheld action and the physical page shape the relation the reader supplies.
