Title: From frame to sequence

[Read HTML](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/creative-methods/from-frame-to-sequence/)

Author: Harsh Joshi

A compelling frame offers a relationship worth keeping: a figure's scale, an opening around it, a line of light. Following it into another image asks which parts can vary while the person and place remain recognisable. Following those images onto a page asks what the reader can infer between them.

That progression changes the unit of the work. The individual image becomes a continuous presence, then a sequence whose meaning exceeds any one frame. What began as a visual choice becomes an experience someone else completes.
