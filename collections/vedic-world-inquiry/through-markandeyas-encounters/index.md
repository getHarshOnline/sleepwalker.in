Title: Through Markandeya's encounters

[Read HTML](https://sleepwalker.in/collections/vedic-world-inquiry/through-markandeyas-encounters/)

Author: Harsh Joshi

An immense revelation can seem like the end of a story. In the *Bhāgavata*, Markandeya's encounter with the cosmic child continues into another meeting, another setting and a request. Reading the adjacent chapters together lets the later response change what the earlier spectacle means.

The first article stays with the immense world, and the second follows what the witness does next. The order matters because seeing more does not settle what the witness wants, or how he relates to what he has seen.

The encounter also leaves me with a question about description: what happens when the world no longer fits the terms in which a witness understood it? Causality takes up [that question in mathematical terms](https://causality.in/collections/changing-descriptions/when-the-description-must-change/), asking which distinctions a description can express and when it needs to change. Here I am reading a theological encounter, with its own meaning and ending; the question can travel without turning the encounter into a mathematical claim.
