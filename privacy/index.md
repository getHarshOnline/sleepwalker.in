# Privacy notice

RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited (RTEPL) is responsible for deciding how personal data is used on [rawthoughts.in](https://rawthoughts.in/), [causality.in](https://causality.in/), [sleepwalker.in](https://sleepwalker.in/) and [post-agi-economics.org](https://post-agi-economics.org/), and on their subdomains, including the documentation at docs.rawthoughts.in and docs.causality.in. RTEPL publishes and operates these websites. This notice explains the information handled when you read these websites or use their contact and reading tools.

<a id="visiting-the-website"></a>

## Visiting the website

GitHub Pages hosts these publications. Serving a page involves connection information, including your IP address and the page requested. GitHub [logs visitors’ IP addresses for security](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection), including when a visitor is not signed in. GitHub handles that information under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and may process it outside India.

Mintlify hosts the documentation at docs.rawthoughts.in and docs.causality.in. Serving those pages involves the same connection information, which Mintlify handles under its [privacy policy](https://www.mintlify.com/legal/privacy) and may process outside India. Mintlify's own visitor telemetry is turned off for these sites. Mintlify also provides those two sites' search, page tools and display settings, under the same privacy policy; the in-browser search, assistant tools, display settings and passage links described below belong to the other websites.

On the other websites, search runs in your browser against each website's own index, so what you type is not sent to a search service. Your search is kept in the search page's address, so like any address it can reach GitHub with a page request, appear in your browser history and go with the address if you share it.

If your browser has an assistant that supports it, each page offers that assistant two read-only tools: the text of the page you are reading and the same in-browser search. They work on what the page already shows and send nothing to us; what the assistant does with what it reads follows its provider's terms.

<a id="contacting-us"></a>

## Contacting us

A contact link opens a new message in your own email application. The website does not receive or store the message as a form submission. If you choose to send it, your email service and the recipient's mail service handle the correspondence. Any professional work that follows has its own correspondence and agreement.

For privacy questions, a grievance or a request concerning your personal data, write to <info@rtepl.co.in>. Include enough information to identify the request, but do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We respond within 30 days and may ask for proportionate verification before disclosing or changing personal data.

<a id="reading-preferences"></a>

## Reading preferences

The display settings remember your colour and motion choices in your browser on this device. You can change them in Settings or clear the website's stored data through your browser. These preferences are used to display the page; they stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

<a id="asking-about-a-passage"></a>

## Asking about a passage

When you select text and choose a reading assistant, the link opens that provider with the selected passage, nearby context, the section and article titles, the publication's name, and the article and publication addresses. The passage is limited to 4,096 characters and the nearby context to 1,024. Selecting text alone prepares the link in your browser; choosing the provider sends it there.

The assistant is an external service. The request is included in its link, so it can also appear in your browser history. The provider's own terms, privacy policy and your account settings govern what it does with the request and any conversation that follows. Reading the article does not require using an assistant.

<a id="how-long-information-is-kept"></a>

## How long information is kept

We keep no record of visits on our own systems; GitHub and Mintlify keep their connection logs for the periods their policies set. Correspondence you send is kept for as long as the matter it concerns needs it and as the law requires.

<a id="security-and-incidents"></a>

## Security and incidents

These websites hold no accounts and receive no form submissions. If a personal-data breach affects you, we inform you and the authorities as applicable law requires.

<a id="your-choices"></a>

## Your choices

Following another external link takes you to that service under its own data practices.

You may ask us about your data, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent or raise a concern using the contact above. Requests are handled under applicable law; information needed to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a dispute may need to be kept. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that has already occurred lawfully.

Changes to the website's data practices will be reflected in this notice. A new optional use that requires consent will ask for that consent rather than treating continued reading as agreement.
